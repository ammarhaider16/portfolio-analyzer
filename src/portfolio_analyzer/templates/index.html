Import holdings

Use columns symbol,quantity,price,asset_type,sector,previous_close. Only symbol and quantity are required. CASH, USD, and cash-type rows become your cash balance. A cash row uses quantity × price, with price defaulting to 1.

Import a portfolio screenshot

PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 5 MB. Extraction creates an editable draft. Unreadable quantities and cash remain blank for you to fill in.
